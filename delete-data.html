Delete your data — Hexagon Puzzle

For the Google Play app Hexagon Puzzle (com.donkeyeatsbacon.hexagonpuzzle). Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Hexagon Puzzle has no accounts and no sign-in. Almost everything it stores stays on your own device, and you can erase it yourself without asking anyone. The one exception is a solution name, if you chose to submit one.

How to delete everything held on your device

1Erase it yourself, immediately

On Android: Settings → Apps → Hexagon → Storage → Clear storage. Uninstalling the app does the same thing. In a browser, clear site data for the game's page.

This erases your collection of solved puzzles, your unfinished board, your sound preference and the record of whether you have seen the tutorial. It is immediate, it needs no request, and none of it was ever sent to us.

How to delete a solution name you submitted

2Email us and we will remove it

Write to rosterhelperadmin@gmail.com

Include the name you submitted, or the solution code it was given — a code looks like 1UNW-ERTE-WLT and is shown on the solved screen and beside the solution in your collection. Either is enough to find it.

We will remove it within 30 days, usually much sooner. You do not need to give a reason, and you do not need to prove it was yours.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your collection, unfinished board, and settings Deleted by you, instantly. Held only on your device; we never receive it and cannot delete it for you.
A solution name you submitted, once approved Deleted on request. Removed from the published list, so it stops appearing for other players.
The public review entry created when you submitted Deleted on request. Submissions create a publicly visible entry for review; we delete that too.
Your IP address Nothing to delete. Seen only while a submission is rate-limited, never stored with the name and never used to identify anyone.

We keep no accounts, no profiles, no analytics and no advertising data, so there is nothing else of yours to retain or erase.

One thing we cannot undo

A name that was already published may have been seen, copied or cached by other people before you asked us to remove it. We can stop showing it; we cannot retrieve copies other people already have. Please avoid putting your real name or anything identifying into a solution name.
