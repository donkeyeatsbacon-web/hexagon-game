⬡ How to play

Fit all 14 pieces into the hexagon — every cell filled, nothing overlapping.

  • ✋
    Drag a piece Move it onto the board. It may hang over the edge while you work.
  • 👆
    Tap a piece Rotates it around the exact hexagon you tapped, so it stays put.
  • ⏳
    Press and hold Opens a menu to rotate either way, or flip the piece over.
  • ◎
    Drag the outer ring Turns the whole board in 60° steps, pieces and all.
  • ⚪
    White flashing cells Two pieces overlap there, or a piece hangs off the board — move it.
