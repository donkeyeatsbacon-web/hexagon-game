Privacy Policy — Hexagon Puzzle

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Hexagon Puzzle is a single-player puzzle game, available as a website and as a mobile app. This policy describes exactly what the game does with data. It is short because the game collects very little.

In brief. There are no accounts and no advertising. Nothing you do in the game is tracked or analysed. The only information that ever leaves your device is a solution name, and only if you choose to submit one — and that name is intended to be published.

What is stored on your device

The game saves the following in your browser or app storage. This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is not accessible to anyone else.

WhatWhy
Your collection of solved solutionsSo you can browse puzzles you have solved
Your in-progress boardSo a half-finished puzzle survives closing the app
Whether you have seen the tutorialSo it is not shown every time
Your sound on/off preferenceTo remember your choice

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage, clearing site data in your browser, or uninstalling the app. None of it is recoverable by us.

Naming a solution (optional)

When you solve the puzzle you may propose a name for that solution. This is entirely optional — the game is fully playable without ever submitting one.

If you do submit a name, the following is sent to a server we operate:

  • The name you typed
  • The solution's identifier and number, and the arrangement of pieces that produced it

Your IP address is visible to that server as part of the ordinary mechanics of an internet request. It is used only to rate-limit submissions so the service cannot be flooded, and it is not stored alongside your name or used to build any profile of you.

Submitted names are published. A submission creates a public entry for review. If the name is approved it becomes visible to every other player of the game. Please do not put personal information — your real name, an email address, or anything identifying — into a solution name.

Moderation

No submitted name appears in the game until it has been reviewed and approved by the developer. Names are reviewed before publication, not after. A name that is offensive, abusive, or contains personal information is simply rejected and never published.

If you believe a published name is objectionable, or you want a name you submitted removed, contact us at the address below and it will be taken down.

What the game does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or passwords
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No third-party trackers or social media SDKs
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties for marketing

The mobile app requests only two permissions: internet access, used solely to submit and retrieve solution names, and vibration, used for touch feedback while playing.

Service providers

Two services are involved in handling a submitted name. They act on our behalf and process only what is described above:

  • Cloudflare — hosts the small service that receives a submission and applies rate limiting.
  • GitHub — hosts the website and stores submitted names for review.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because solution names are free text, every name is reviewed by a person before it can be seen by other players. If you believe a child has submitted personal information in a name, contact us and it will be removed.

Your rights

Data held on your device is under your control and can be deleted at any time as described above. For a name you have submitted, you may ask us to delete it — contact us with the name or solution number and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date above.

Contact

Questions, removal requests, or reports of objectionable content: donkeyeatsbacon@gmail.com
